# Commercial licence

LiveActionAOV is free for individuals, freelancers, studios under 10 people
and non-commercial use (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). Larger studios need a
commercial licence.

## What you get

- A **perpetual** licence for one major version (for example every 1.x
  release), for the whole studio: no seat counting, no licence server, no
  phone-home.
- The software **as-is**. LiveActionAOV is built and maintained by one
  person: bugs get fixed, but with no guaranteed response time.
- Upgrading to the next major version is optional.
